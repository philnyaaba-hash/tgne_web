# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The TGNE business owner uses the admin dashboard to review client requests, manage quotes and work status, and keep client activity visible. Corporate clients use a separate portal to submit and track service orders, review quotes and invoices, and contact TGNE.

## Product Purpose

Give TGNE and its corporate clients a clear shared view of service work, from request through delivery. The admin view should surface the work that needs attention; the client view should make order status and client actions easy to find.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, and trustworthy. Keep the interface direct and calm so work status and next actions are easy to understand.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered, generic SaaS dashboards and decorative effects that compete with work information.

## Design Principles

- Put urgent work and next actions where users can see them quickly.
- Keep admin operations and corporate client tasks distinct.
- Use clear, consistent labels for order stages and financial status.
- Authenticated dashboards must use persisted service and account data; when the database is unavailable, show an actionable error instead of simulating a successful submission.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, keyboard-operable interactions, visible focus states, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
